Fuel Buddy

Helping vehicle owners compare real-time fuel prices, locate nearby stations, and access emergency roadside assistance.

01

Project Overview

Fuel Buddy is a mobile app concept designed to solve three interconnected problems that vehicle owners face daily - unpredictable fuel prices, difficulty locating nearby stations, and lack of quick emergency roadside help. This was a solo concept project where I owned the full design process from research to final UI.

Role Solo UI/UX Designer (End to End)
Timeline 4 Weeks - Concept Project
Platform Mobile App (iOS & Android)
Tools Figma · Notion · Pen & Paper
02

Problem Statement

Fuel prices fluctuate daily, yet most vehicle owners have no easy way to compare prices before refueling. Beyond cost, finding a nearby station in an unfamiliar area and getting quick help during a breakdown are equally frustrating pain points with no unified solution.

No Price Transparency

70% of drivers refuel without checking prices, leading to unnecessary overspend on every fill.

Station Discovery Gap

Google Maps shows stations but not live prices. Users have to cross-reference multiple apps.

Emergency Assistance Void

40% of breakdowns are preventable - but when they happen, drivers struggle to find quick help.

Fleet Inefficiency

Fleet operators have no visibility into driver fueling behaviour or station price optimisation.

Design Process

I followed a structured design thinking approach - moving from broad ideation to focused execution through four phases.

Informal user interviews + competitive research to validate the problem space.

Brainstorming & Ideation

I used divergent thinking to explore ideas across four categories before converging on the most valuable features.

Price & Savings

  • Crowdsourced fuel prices from users
  • AI-based price prediction
  • Cashback & rewards programme
  • Fuel subscription discount plans

Delivery & Convenience

  • On-demand fuel delivery to location
  • Emergency fuel top-up mid-journey
  • Station pre-booking at locked price

Vehicle & Roadside

  • 24/7 emergency assistance
  • AI vehicle health monitor
  • Subscription-based roadside plans

Navigation & Community

  • Driver community & station reviews
  • AI traffic-based fuel planning
08

Core Features Designed

From the brainstorming pool, I converged on 5 features that deliver maximum value with the highest user need and lowest implementation risk.

01

Real-Time Fuel Price Comparison

Users search and compare live prices across nearby stations. Saves money on every refuel.

02

Nearby Station Finder

Map-first interface showing closest stations with price overlays and distance. Built for unfamiliar areas.

03

Instant Price Alerts

Push notifications when prices drop or spike in the user's area. Helps users time their refuel.

04

On-Demand Roadside Assistance

One-tap access to puncture repair, towing, and oil refills with real-time ETA tracking.

05

Trip Cost Calculator

Users enter destination and vehicle type - app estimates total fuel cost based on live prices en route.

10

Key Design Decisions

These three decisions were intentional and grounded in user behaviour.

→

Map-First Home Screen

Chose a map view as the default instead of a list because users in unfamiliar areas need spatial context first. A list requires mental mapping - the map is immediate.

→

Price as the Hero Element

Fuel price is displayed in large, high-contrast text on every station card. This removes friction from the core task - comparing prices at a glance.

→

One-Tap Emergency Access

Roadside assistance is accessible from a persistent bottom bar, not buried in a menu. In an emergency, users should not have to think - they need to tap once and get help.

11

Future Scope

With more time and real user testing, I would prioritise these next-phase features.

Fleet Management Dashboard

B2B view for fleet managers - track all vehicles, fuel spend per driver, and flag overpriced refueling. Unlocks a high-value commercial market segment.

AI Price Prediction

"Prices in your area likely to rise tomorrow - refuel today to save ₹3/litre." Based on historical trend data. Low-effort, high-perceived-value feature.

OBD Low Fuel Integration

Connect to the car's fuel sensor via OBD dongle - app auto-alerts when fuel is low and shows the nearest cheapest station. True proactive UX.

Scheduled Fuel Delivery

Not just emergencies - users can schedule morning fuel delivery to home or office. Think Swiggy model applied to fuel logistics.

12

Reflection & Learnings

✦

Define success metrics before you design

I didn't set measurable goals at the start. Going forward, I'd define what success looks like - even rough proxies - before touching Figma.

✦

Talk to end users, not just stakeholders

I worked from secondary research and assumptions. Even 3 quick user interviews would have sharpened the problem definition significantly.

✦

Design for the B2B angle early

The fleet management use case emerged late. Identifying it earlier would have shaped core architecture decisions differently.
